Mobilization regimens and formats

Four regimens trade off yield, timing and donor burden. Dual mobilization (Reg H) enhances CD34+ yield across all donor types.

CD34+

From collection to isolated HSPCs

Isolated CD34+ cells

CD34+ cells are isolated from any mobilized leukapheresis collection by positive immunomagnetic separation: CD34+ beads bind the target cells and unwanted cells are washed away.

Viable CD34+ counts are quantified by the ISHAGE four-parameter flow cytometry protocol on request.

Made-to-order

Custom donor requests, mobilization regimens, bulk isolations and cryopreservation are available. Everything is made on-site, from donor procurement through shipment.
