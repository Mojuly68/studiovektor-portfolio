Capabilities & equipment

Check your part against 41 machines before you call.

Enter your part's bounding box. Mills check X, Y and Z travel; lathes read the largest cross-section as diameter and L as length; laser and waterjet read L × W as the sheet and H as thickness in steel.

Part envelopeExample part
MachineTypeCapacityNotesYour part

Capacities come from AMT's October 2025 equipment list. Travel is not the same as usable part size: tool length, workholding and fixtures take room, most of all in Z. A "fits" result means the part is inside travel; we confirm the real setup during DFM review, and oversize parts still get quoted.
