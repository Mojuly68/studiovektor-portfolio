Method

Isolation and applications

  • Positive immunomagnetic separation. Beads bind the target cells, which are retained while non-target cells are washed away.
  • Negative immunomagnetic separation. Beads bind non-target cells, which are depleted, leaving the unlabeled cells of interest.
  • Quality control. Cell count and viability checked with a validated acridine orange / propidium iodide (AO/PI) protocol.
