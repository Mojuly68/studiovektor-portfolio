Storage and thawing of cryopreserved vials

Ship and handle on dry ice, store in LN2 vapor phase, thaw at 37 °C. Transient warming during transfer can reduce post-thaw viability and function.

StoreLN2 vapor phaseThaw37 °C
Storage PDFThawing PDF
Storage

Storage

Keep frozen in transit

Products ship and are handled on dry ice.

Inspect

On arrival, check vials for loose caps or other defects.

Avoid warming

Keep vials completely frozen during handling and transfer; avoid ambient exposure.

Store

Use immediately or store in the vapor phase of an LN2 tank.

Until use

Keep frozen cells on dry ice until ready to thaw.

WARNINGDo not store AllCells® frozen cell products in the liquid phase of LN2. Liquid can enter screw-top vials and make them explode on removal. Liquid-phase, −80 °C or −20 °C storage voids the warranty.
Thawing

Thawing procedure

Warm

Confirm the vial is tightly capped and place it in a 37 °C water bath.

Swirl

Swirl gently until only a small ice crystal remains.

Disinfect

Remove the vial, wipe with 70% ethanol and move it into a biosafety cabinet.

Dilute

Transfer the whole vial into a tube with at least an equal volume of thawing medium (RPMI 1640, IMDM or DMEM + 10% heat-inactivated FBS, 20–25 °C). Higher concentrations may need more.

Rinse

Rinse the vial with 1 mL fresh thawing medium and add it to the tube.

Count

Count immediately. See the counting protocol.

Wash

Centrifuge before any downstream assay to remove remaining cryoprotectant.

Safety

Safety precautions

Health hazards of liquid nitrogen

LN2 expands about 700-fold, which can burst cryovials, containers or equipment. Contact with LN2 or cold vapor causes severe tissue damage. Nitrogen displaces oxygen and can cause dizziness, nausea, unconsciousness and death.

  • Wear waterproof, thermal-insulated cryo gloves (never submerge hands), lab coat, long pants, closed-toe shoes and chemical splash goggles
  • Supplied cryovials are for vapor-phase storage only
  • For liquid-phase storage use approved liquid-phase cryovials, or seal gas-phase vials in an LN2-rated outer envelope
  • Handle cryovials in a biosafety cabinet or chemical hood with the sash lowered
WARRANTYFrozen cell products are warranted for 1 year from receipt when stored properly. Storage in LN2 liquid phase, −80 °C or −20 °C voids the warranty.
CAUTIONFor Research Use Only. Not for clinical diagnostic or therapeutic use. Treat every product as potentially contaminated biological material, even with negative serology.
