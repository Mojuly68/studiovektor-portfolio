Chain of custody

Donor to dock, under one roof.

Collection, processing and pack-out happen on-site, so material reaches you fresh and with a complete record. Scroll through the five handoffs.

01/05
CheckpointPhysical exam + health questionnaire
01 · Qualify

Every donor is screened before collection.

Donors complete a physical exam and a health questionnaire. Consent is IRB-approved for both research and commercial use.

  • Infectious disease screening at multiple time points
  • Backup and parallel donors held for critical orders
02 · Collect

Leukapheresis at our own centers.

Mononuclear cells are collected on the Spectra Optia Apheresis System by qualified clinicians. Mobilized collections follow G-CSF, plerixafor or dual regimens.

  • Leukopaks sized by volume or total nucleated cell count
  • Bone marrow aspirates of 10, 25, 50 and 100 mL
03 · Isolate

Target cells separated within hours.

Integrated labs run immunomagnetic separation soon after collection. Sequential isolation recovers a second population from the same donor's unlabeled fraction.

  • CD34+, T, NK, B cells, monocytes and PBMCs
  • Viable CD34+ counts by ISHAGE flow protocol on request
04 · Cryopreserve

Banked for comparable lots.

Single-donor isolations can be cryopreserved and banked, so later experiments draw on the same consistent cell source.

  • GMP cryopreserved leukopaks made in an ISO 7 cleanroom
  • Made-to-stock and custom cryopreservation
05 · Ship

Packed to arrive on your schedule.

Temperature-controlled AeroSafe Global packaging, validated to ISTA standards, with a temperature logger in every shipment.

  • Same-day, next-day or specific-date pickup
  • Shipping guidance ahead of severe weather
